METHODOLOGICAL INFORMATION
--------------------------

1. Description of methods used for collection/generation of data: 

Each cross made in a crop in a given year was recorded including the parents and sometimes number of seeds produced.  

The designation for a cross consists of the first and last letter of the common name of the crop followed by a number that includes the last two digits of the year and then the sequential number of the cross within that year, e.g. AE 5501 would be the first cross (01) made in 1955 (55) in the crop apple (AE).  Grape is GE, strawberry is SY, raspberry is RY etc.
